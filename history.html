THE BAND

History

From Irvine to the stage

The group formed in 2004 under the name The Jakes and later adopted the name Young the Giant. Their self-titled debut album arrived in 2010 and introduced listeners to My Body and Cough Syrup.

Album milestones

  • 2010 — Young the Giant
  • 2014 — Mind Over Matter
  • 2016 — Home of the Strange
  • 2018 — Mirror Master
  • 2022 — American Bollywood

Band members

Sameer Gadhia, Jacob Tilley, Eric Cannata, Payam Doostzadeh and François Comtois make up the band’s longtime lineup.
