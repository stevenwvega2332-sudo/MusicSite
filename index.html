Young the Giant

Meet the band

Young the Giant is an American indie rock band from Irvine, California. Their music combines expressive vocals, melodic guitars and energetic rhythms. From the breakout single Cough Syrup to the reflective Mind Over Matter, their songs explore identity, relationships and change.
